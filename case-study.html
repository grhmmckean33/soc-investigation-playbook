Portfolio case study

Creator Partnership Phishing Email Investigation

An evidence led analysis of an email claiming to represent Shure Media, including header validation, IOC enrichment, Microsoft Defender scope hunting and user impact review.

Likely phishingMedium confidenceSeverity: MediumNo confirmed compromise

Executive summary

The email used a partnership and sponsorship lure, a Gmail sender address, Shure branding and a call to action link hosted on shor[.]by. SPF and DKIM passed, but authentication only proved that Gmail authorised the sender—not that the sender represented Shure. No malicious URL reputation, credential submission, malware execution or account compromise was confirmed.

The recipient confirmed that the message was opened and read. Microsoft Defender searches found no matching email or click telemetry, likely because the mailbox was outside the monitored tenant. The final verdict remained Likely Phishing with Medium Confidence.

5Ws and 1H

Who

Unknown external sender using the display name “Shure Media”; known recipient was a MyDFIR mailbox.

What

A sponsorship themed email encouraged access to an external “Creator Dashboard”.

When

Sent 26 May 2026 20:23:39 +0300 and received 26 May 2026 13:23:54 -0400.

Where

Email channel, recipient mailbox and external CTA infrastructure at shor[.]by.

Why

Exact objective unknown. The observable goal was to persuade the user to interact with external infrastructure.

How

Brand association, financial incentive, trust building language and a prominent CTA link.

Scope hunting evidence

Three Defender Advanced Hunting searches were completed for subject, sender and related campaign themes. All returned no results.

Campaign theme search
EmailEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(35d)
| where SenderFromDomain =~ "gmail.com"
| where Subject has_any ("Collab", "Creator", "Partnership", "Shure")
| project Timestamp, SenderFromAddress, RecipientEmailAddress, Subject
| order by Timestamp desc
Limitation: No Defender telemetry does not prove the email was not delivered. It means the message was not observed in the monitored tenant or available retention window.

Lessons learned

01

Authentication is not legitimacy

SPF and DKIM can pass for attacker controlled Gmail accounts.

02

Unknown is not malicious

Threat intelligence results must be recorded exactly as returned.

03

Scope every alert

Negative search results are still evidence when queries and limitations are documented.

04

Separate actions from recommendations

Analyst tasks belong under actions taken; recommendations should improve lasting controls.

05

Defang IOCs

Every indicator in a report should be rendered non clickable to prevent accidental interaction.

Control focused recommendations

  1. Strengthen email controls for display name and brand impersonation from free mail providers.
  2. Alert on external CTA links using redirect or short link services when paired with sponsorship, partnership or creator program themes.
  3. Provide targeted awareness guidance for influencer, collaboration and sponsorship lures.
  4. Maintain a repeatable scope hunting procedure and retain query evidence with each phishing case.
  5. Require IOC defanging in SOC reports, tickets, emails and knowledge base articles.
